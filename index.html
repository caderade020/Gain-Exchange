How the score works

Fitness index = a 0–100 blend of four parts: Consistency (30%: workout days per week vs your weekly goal, last 4 weeks), Strength progress (30%: how far your best recent lift, hold, or rep count is above your first session, averaged across movements with 2+ sessions), Split balance (20%: how many of 6 muscle groups you hit and how even upper vs lower days are), and Activity (20%: active minutes per week vs 150, plus how often you hit your step goal). Parts with no data yet are left out. This is a motivation tool, not a medical measure.
